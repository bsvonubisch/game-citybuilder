# Wild berries and gathering

Added November 18, 2026. Existing saves receive bushes once, without changing
buildings, roads, tree positions, inventories or progress. `berriesVersion: 1`
prevents a reload from restoring removed bushes or restarting fruit timers.
Only empty dry woodland cells are eligible, with spacing from roads/buildings
and deposits. New starter towns also include a hut at (33,26), facing northwest;
existing towns get bushes only and the player chooses where to build their hut.

The 1×1 gatherer's hut costs 100 gold, 6 wood, 3 stone and 100 construction labor.
Two workers search within 10 tiles. They commute to work, walk between natural
tree trunks, pick 6 berries in 6 productive seconds, and carry the basket home.
Only returning cargo enters inventory. Bushes replenish in 1,920 ticks (two
game days, eight minutes at normal speed). Fences, water, buildings, planted
trees and steep ground block access. Night stops new trips; existing trips finish.
Tools use the same productivity and wear rules as other workplaces.

Berries count as food. Local deliveries move them to warehouses/markets; homes
collect their food from these stores without trucks. No exports. Selecting a
bush shows ripe/regrowing status, a timer and the relevant hut/build action.
The hut shows reachable bushes, worker activity, storage and its placement range.

## Assets and projection

- `building-gatherer.png`: transparent 1254×1254 atlas, 2×2 cells of 627 pixels;
  SW entrance, SE entrance, NE rear, NW rear in row order.
- Source reference: `art-guides/gatherer-guide.png`, generated by
  `scripts/gatherer-guide.mjs`; paint prompt in `gatherer-prompt.txt`.
- `building-art.ts` contains ground anchors. `resolvedBuildingArt` measures
  real lower contacts, applies one uniform scale across all four views, and
  places the upright art on the shared 4-pixel stone foundation. No distortion.
- `berry-bushes-v2.png`: transparent 1254×1254, 3×3 cells of 418 pixels.
  Frames 0–2 ripe, 3–5 picked, 6–8 berry baskets. Three bush variants.
  Bush height 19 world pixels; carried basket width 7. Berry prompt stored in
  `berry-bushes-prompt.txt`. V1's opaque background was rejected.
- Picking reuses the existing farm worker bend/reach frames at 22-pixel body
  height. Travel uses the standard directional walk cycle. Foot anchors remain
  fixed; carried baskets attach to the worker rather than their destination.
