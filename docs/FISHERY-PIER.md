# Fishery timber

The dock now uses the current woodland bridge timber, replacing the older bright
orange wood sheet. `timber-material.ts` shares the deck crop with bridges and
defines the opaque timber samples for the dock rim, pilings and mooring posts.

Maintain six boards per tile and a continuous two-tile material repeat. Boards
cross the short dock dimension and rotate with the fishery. The land foundation,
water footprint, deck height, boat berths and routing are unchanged.

Placed plots, build ghosts and catalogue/details thumbnails all use the same
material. Checked beside a timber bridge in all four camera directions, including
the two views where the house occludes part of the dock. The production build
passes and the browser reports no errors.
